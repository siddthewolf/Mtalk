# Privacy Policy — Relic Rush

_Last updated: 2026-06-25_

Relic Rush is a single-player offline game. We respect your privacy.

## Information we collect

**None.** Relic Rush does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any
personal information. The app:

- Requests no Android permissions.
- Has no account system, login, or registration.
- Does not contain analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.
- Makes no network connections.

## Local data

Your high score is stored **only on your device** using Android's local
`SharedPreferences`. It never leaves your device and is removed if you uninstall
the app.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including
children under 13.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a
new "Last updated" date.
